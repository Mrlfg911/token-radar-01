How a row is priced

Four multipliers on the protocol's TVL. Every one of them is stated on the row.

  1. Category multiplewhat a dollar of TVL in this business is worth at launch, from the category's own table
  2. Growth1 + the seven-day move × weight, clipped so one wild week cannot carry a row
  3. Maturitya protocol that has run years with no token earns more than one listed last month
  4. Chain spreadeach extra chain the protocol actually runs on, up to four

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain spread
